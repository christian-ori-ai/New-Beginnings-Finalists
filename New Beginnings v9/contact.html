A $100 reservation fee secures your date. The fee is refundable up to 90 days before your event.

Yes. New Beginnings is licensed and insured, and documentation can be provided for venues that require it.

Can we customize playlists and requests?

Absolutely. We collaborate on must-play and do-not-play lists and adjust in real time to your crowd.

Yes, travel is available for nearby regions depending on date and venue logistics.
