Wedding Day Coordination

Focused on a stress free day.

This package is ideal for the Bride and Groom that have all the wedding planning and preparation under control. Wedding Day Coordination is focused on a stress free day, giving the couple the opportunity to show up, say "I Do," and enjoy their special day.

The Day of Coordinator handles behind-the-scenes details and ensures your wedding day goes smoothly so you and your families are free to celebrate.

What We Coordinate

  • Vendor coordination and management
  • Ceremony and reception timeline execution
  • Wedding rehearsal support with officiant and wedding party
  • Guest and vendor question handling
  • Issue resolution on event day
  • Day-of emergency kit support
